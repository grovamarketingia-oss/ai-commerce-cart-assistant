# AI Commerce Cart Assistant

AI Commerce Cart Assistant is an AI-powered conversational sales system designed for retail and e-commerce businesses.

The system assists customers directly through WhatsApp, understands their needs, recommends relevant products and generates a personalized shopping cart connected to the online store.

Its purpose is to reduce friction in the purchase journey, improve customer experience and increase conversion rates.

## Business Problem

Retail and e-commerce businesses frequently lose sales due to:

- Slow response times
- Unanswered product questions
- Complex purchase journeys
- Customer uncertainty before checkout
- Limited product guidance
- Lack of follow-up
- Abandoned sales opportunities
- Friction between WhatsApp conversations and the online store

AI Commerce Cart Assistant addresses these challenges by connecting conversational customer service with product recommendations and cart generation.

## Core Capabilities

The system can:

- Provide customer service through WhatsApp
- Identify customer needs and purchase intent
- Answer product-related questions
- Recommend relevant products
- Personalize product suggestions
- Guide customers through the purchase decision
- Generate a shopping cart
- Send a direct checkout link
- Reduce friction between conversation and purchase
- Capture customer information
- Register commercial opportunities
- Support follow-up workflows
- Facilitate abandoned-cart recovery strategies
- Escalate conversations to human staff when required
