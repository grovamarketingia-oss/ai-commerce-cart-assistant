<h1>AI Commerce Cart Assistant</h1> <p><strong>AI Commerce Cart Assistant</strong> is an AI-powered conversational sales system designed for retail and e-commerce businesses.</p> <p>The system assists customers directly through WhatsApp, understands their needs, recommends relevant products and generates a personalized shopping cart connected to the online store.</p> <p>Its purpose is to reduce friction in the purchase journey, improve customer experience and increase conversion rates.</p> <h2>Business Problem</h2> <p>Retail and e-commerce businesses frequently lose sales due to:</p> <ul> <li>Slow response times</li> <li>Unanswered product questions</li> <li>Complex purchase journeys</li> <li>Customer uncertainty before checkout</li> <li>Limited product guidance</li> <li>Lack of follow-up</li> <li>Abandoned sales opportunities</li> <li>Friction between WhatsApp conversations and the online store</li> </ul> <p>AI Commerce Cart Assistant addresses these challenges by connecting conversational customer service with product recommendations and cart generation.</p> <h2>Core Capabilities</h2> <ul> <li>Provide customer service through WhatsApp</li> <li>Identify customer needs and purchase intent</li> <li>Answer product-related questions</li> <li>Recommend relevant products</li> <li>Personalize product suggestions</li> <li>Guide customers through the purchase decision</li> <li>Generate a shopping cart</li> <li>Send a direct checkout link</li> <li>Reduce friction between conversation and purchase</li> <li>Capture customer information</li> <li>Register commercial opportunities</li> <li>Support follow-up workflows</li> <li>Facilitate abandoned-cart recovery strategies</li> <li>Escalate conversations to human staff when required</li> </ul> <h2>Customer Journey</h2> <pre>Customer inquiry → needs identification → product recommendation → pre-purchase support → cart generation → direct checkout link → purchase follow-up → data capture and optimization</pre> <h2>Business Impact</h2> <ul> <li>Faster response times</li> <li>Improved customer experience</li> <li>Higher conversion rates</li> <li>Increased cart generation</li> <li>Reduced purchase friction</li> <li>Better commercial follow-up</li> <li>Improved lead visibility</li> <li>Greater recovery of sales opportunities</li> <li>Increased revenue from digital channels</li> </ul> <h2>Use Cases</h2> <ul> <li>Fashion</li> <li>Beauty and personal care</li> <li>Wellness products</li> <li>Food and beverage</li> <li>Consumer products</li> <li>Specialty retail</li> <li>Businesses
